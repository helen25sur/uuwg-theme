Our story

Join us

Our story began in 2017 as a small club of Ukrainian women in Greece, created to build community, preserve culture, and support one another. Following the outbreak of war in Ukraine in 2022, our efforts grew significantly as we expanded our humanitarian and social support to respond to urgent needs.

In 2025, this work was formalized with our official registration as a non-governmental organization under the name Union of Ukrainian Women in Greece, marking a new chapter in our mission and impact.
